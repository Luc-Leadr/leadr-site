Into Switzerland

How to find a distributor in Switzerland

Selling in Switzerland does not always mean setting up a company there. A well-chosen distributor already knows your customers, speaks their language and handles delivery and service. We identify them, qualify them and introduce them to you.

Book a meetingSee the approach

Who is it for?

Manufacturers whose offer sells through a network.

French manufacturers and software publishers, SMEs and mid-sized companies, whose offer sells through resellers, installers or integrators: technical equipment, construction, industry, professional products.

Our approach

Six steps, from scoping to follow-up.

  1. 1

    Scope your offer

    Choose the products to offer first, the terms you are prepared to grant and the profile of the distributor you are looking for.

  2. 2

    Map the market

    List the distributors, wholesalers and integrators active in your sector, in French- and German-speaking Switzerland, then qualify them.
    What you receive: a qualified market map, distributor by distributor.

  3. 3

    Present your offer

    Approach the most relevant ones in their language, gauge their interest and gather their objections.
    What you receive: feedback from the field on your prices, your standards, expectations and competitors already present.

  4. 4

    Organise the meetings

    Bring your teams together with the most interested distributors, in Switzerland or remotely.
    What you receive: prepared meetings with people who already know your offer.

  5. 5

    Support the agreement

    Prepare the negotiation alongside you. The distribution agreement is concluded directly between you and your distributor.
    What you receive: ongoing support through to signature.

  6. 6

    Remain your point of contact

    For a period agreed together, we remain your point of contact on the ground and the preferred link between you and your distributor, according to what you expect from us.

We work for the manufacturer, never for the distributor.

We do not buy your products, we do not store them and we do not sell them on your behalf. Our role is to find the right partner, not the first one to say yes.

Frequently asked questions

Finding a distributor: your questions.

What exclusivity should you grant the distributor?

It is not automatic. Exclusivity is negotiated by region, by product range and for a limited period, often linked to sales targets.

What documentation is needed in German?

In German-speaking Switzerland, your product sheets, terms and presentation materials must be available in German. We support you in adapting them, so that they genuinely speak to German-speaking distributors rather than simply being translated.

Who drafts the distribution agreement?

A lawyer or legal adviser specialising in the Swiss market, whom we put you in touch with. We follow the whole process through to signature.

What if no distributor is a good fit?

We tell you, and we explain why. The feedback gathered shows what needs adjusting, or whether another route is better suited.

Which regions of Switzerland do you cover?

French- and German-speaking Switzerland alike, with an approach in each region's language.
