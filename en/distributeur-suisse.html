Into Switzerland

How to find a distributor in Switzerland

Selling in Switzerland does not always mean setting up a company or hiring staff there. A well-chosen distributor already knows your customers, speaks their language and handles delivery and service. We find them for you, introduce them to you and remain your point of contact on the ground.

Book a meetingSee the approach

Who is it for?

Manufacturers whose products sell through specialist distributors.

This approach is designed for French manufacturers (SMEs and mid-sized companies) that already sell their products in France through distributors, wholesalers or installers and want to replicate the model in Switzerland.

For example, a manufacturer of heating or water treatment equipment that sells to installers through wholesalers, a machinery manufacturer that works through specialist resellers, or a business software publisher that relies on integrators.

Your products are ready and your price stands up to comparison, but you lack the time and the contacts to find the right partner across the border on your own. That is where we come in.

What is a distributor? A Swiss company that buys your products, stocks them, resells them to its own customers and provides the service. You only have one customer to invoice in Switzerland, and it already knows the market.

Our role

Your partner on the ground in Switzerland, not just an address book.

Our role does not stop at passing on contacts. We prepare your entry into the market, support your distributor's launch and stay present in Switzerland on your behalf. You have no office to open and no one to hire.

No structure to set up

No Swiss company, no office, no one to recruit to launch your sales. Your distributor drives the sales, and we keep everything connected.

Compliant products from day one

Technical standards, federal regulations, requirements specific to each canton: we check what applies to your products before presenting them, so your distributor can sell them without unpleasant surprises.

A contact on the ground

We speak your distributors' language, we know how they work and we meet them whenever needed.

One partner from start to finish

Search, first contact, meetings, support with the agreement alongside a specialist lawyer or legal adviser, then the launch. You do not have to coordinate several providers.

Support beyond the launch

For an agreed period, we support your distributor's launch and remain the main link between you and your distributor: regular reviews, day-to-day questions, changes in the rules, new products to introduce.

Our approach

Six steps, from preparation to follow-up.

  1. 1

    Prepare your offer

    Together, choose the products to offer first, check the Swiss standards and rules that apply to them, canton by canton, then set the terms for the distributor.
    What you receive: the list of adjustments to make before selling.

  2. 2

    Identify distributors

    List the distributors, wholesalers and integrators active in your sector, in French- and German-speaking Switzerland, then check which ones match your offer.
    What you receive: a reasoned shortlist, distributor by distributor.

  3. 3

    Present your offer

    Contact the shortlisted distributors in their language, gauge their interest and gather their questions and objections.
    What you receive: their feedback on your products, your prices and the competition already in place.

  4. 4

    Organise the meetings

    Bring your teams together with the most interested distributors, in Switzerland or by video call.
    What you receive: prepared meetings with people who already know your offer.

  5. 5

    Support the agreement

    Prepare the negotiation with you and put you in touch with a lawyer or legal adviser specialising in the Swiss market. The contract is signed directly between you and your distributor.
    What you receive: support through to signature.

  6. 6

    Launch and follow up

    Support your distributor's launch: first orders, presenting your products to its teams, materials in French or German. After that, we remain your point of contact on the ground for an agreed period, in line with your expectations.

We work for the manufacturer, never for the distributor.

We do not buy your products, we do not store them and we do not sell them on your behalf. Our role is to find the right partner, not the first one to say yes.

Frequently asked questions

Finding a distributor: your questions.

What exclusivity should you grant the distributor?

It is not automatic. Exclusivity is negotiated by region, by product range and for a limited period, often linked to sales targets.

What documentation is needed in German?

In German-speaking Switzerland, your product sheets, terms and presentation materials must be available in German. We support you in adapting them, so that they genuinely speak to German-speaking distributors rather than simply being translated.

Who drafts the distribution agreement?

A lawyer or legal adviser specialising in the Swiss market, whom we put you in touch with. We follow the whole process through to signature.

What if no distributor is a good fit?

We tell you, and we explain why. The feedback gathered shows what needs adjusting, or whether another route is better suited.

Which regions of Switzerland do you cover?

French- and German-speaking Switzerland alike, with an approach in each region's language.
