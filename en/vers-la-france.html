The size of the market

The weight of relationships

Networks and referrals often open more doors than the quality of the offer alone.

The decision path

Organisations that are sometimes more hierarchical, with several people to convince.

Business customs

Negotiation, payment terms, formality of exchanges: everything reads differently.

Market standards

French and European standards, certifications, sector regulations: to be checked product by product.

Frequently asked questions

Growing in France: your questions.

How can a Swiss company prospect in France?

By targeting specific regions rather than "France" as a whole. The country is centralised in its laws but very regional in its networks: each economic area has its own decision-makers, trade fairs and partners. We identify the people who matter and introduce you directly.

Do I need to set up a company in France?

Not necessarily at first. Depending on your business, you can sell from Switzerland, work with a sales agent or open a liaison office. VAT and tax representation obligations need to be checked for your case. A French subsidiary makes sense once the business is established.

Which region of France should I start with?

With the one where your target clients are concentrated, not necessarily the closest. Every sector has its hubs: industry, health, energy and food do not share the same geography. The first meeting also helps make that choice.

What are the business differences between France and Switzerland?

In France, decisions often go further up the hierarchy and personal relationships carry a lot of weight. Meetings are easier to secure with a recommendation, and negotiations are often more direct than in Switzerland.
