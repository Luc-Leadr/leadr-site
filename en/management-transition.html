When to call on an interim executive.

Launch the Swiss entity and run it until the permanent hire is in place.

Ensuring continuity

Replace an absent or departed executive without leaving the business without a pilot.

Transforming

Lead a reorganisation, a transfer of activity or a turnaround.

Frequently asked questions

Interim management: your questions.

How long does an interim management assignment last?

As long as your situation requires: a few months to ensure continuity, longer for a start-up or a transformation. The length is set during framing and can change.

Does the interim executive become our employee?

No. They work under an assignment, without an employment contract with your company. Leadr remains your point of contact for follow-up.

Why a Swiss profile?

Because they know the country from the inside: the working language, customs, local employment law and networks. They are operational at once, with no adjustment period.
