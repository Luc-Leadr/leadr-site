Engagement formats

You know what you are buying before you commit.

Four formats, each with a defined scope, duration and written deliverables. They apply to Switzerland, to France and to both Swiss language regions.

Book a meetingWhich format suits you?

01

Market diagnosis

Find out whether your offer has a place, and where to start.

Duration4 to 6 weeks

For you ifYou are considering a new market and want to decide on facts, not impressions.

What you receive

  • An analysis of the market and the competition, including prices
  • The list of standards and rules that apply to your offer
  • A map of the decision-makers and partners to approach
  • A written recommendation: go, wait or adapt, and how
  • A face-to-face debrief with your management

02

Targeted assignment

A precise question, a precise answer.

DurationA few weeks

For you ifYou have a one-off need: checking compliance, meeting the right decision-makers, settling a question of structure.

What you receive

  • A clear written answer, with its sources
  • Where needed, a named list of decision-makers and a prepared approach for each
  • Recommendations your teams can apply directly

03

Permanent antenna

Your commercial presence in the market, month after month.

DurationMonthly, in stages

For you ifYou want meetings and clients in the target market, without hiring or setting up a structure.

What you receive

  • Preparing your offer: messaging, materials in French and German, prices in Swiss francs
  • Prepared introductions to targeted decision-makers
  • Ongoing monitoring of rules, tenders and players in your sector
  • Answers to your questions as soon as they arise
  • A monthly report with figures: contacts, meetings, leads in progress

04

Setting up and local management

When the business justifies a presence on the ground.

DurationTailored

For you ifYour sales are there and you need a structure, a site or local management.

What you receive

  • Choosing the structure, the canton or the region
  • Coordinating the specialists: fiduciary, notary, bank, lawyer
  • Recruiting key people, or interim management through our strategic partnership
  • A shared timetable and a single point of contact until you are set up

Which format suits you?

Start from your situation.

The formats can be combined. A diagnosis or a targeted assignment can lead to a permanent antenna, then to setting up. Each stage is decided on the results of the previous one, with no year-long lock-in.

Frequently asked questions

Engagement formats: your questions.

How much does each format cost?

Each engagement is sized to your project, the target market and the number of regions involved. The diagnosis and the targeted assignment are charged at a fixed fee agreed before starting. The permanent antenna works monthly.

Do I have to start with a diagnosis?

No. If your question is precise, a targeted assignment is enough. If your offer is already adapted to the market, the permanent antenna can start straight away, with a first month devoted to preparation.

Do these formats also apply to France and between Swiss regions?

Yes. The four formats apply to all three directions: from France to Switzerland, from Switzerland to France, and from one Swiss language region to the other.

Who does the work?

Your dedicated point of contact leads the engagement from start to finish, supported by the Leadr team and, where needed, by specialists from our network.
