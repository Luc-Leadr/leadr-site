The Leadr method

A method to lose neither time nor money.

An honest starting point, four steps and one goal: real business in the target market.

First of all

Is your offer exportable?

An offer built on a product, a technology or technical know-how transfers well from one country to another. A business governed by national regulation, such as certain professions in healthcare, law or construction, first needs a specific review.

We check this at the first meeting, and we tell you frankly.

The process

Four steps, each with a concrete result.

  1. 1

    Frame

    We analyse your offer, your goals, your resources and your constraints.
    What you get: a clear diagnosis and an informed decision on what comes next.

  2. 2

    Validate

    We research the standards and texts that apply to your product and test your offer with real buyers in the target market.
    What you get: feedback from the field and a precise list of what needs adapting.

  3. 3

    Build

    We define the roadmap: priority regions, targets, message, and structure if needed.
    What you get: a concrete, prioritised action plan.

  4. 4

    Activate

    We identify the right contacts through analysis of the market and its companies, then approach them directly and introduce them to you.
    What you get: qualified meetings and your first clients.

The four steps do not stop at the first client.

A new regulatory question, a new segment to open, a partner to find: we remain your antenna in the market, available whenever the need arises.

For the long term

18 to 24 months: the real time a market takes.

Establishing a lasting presence in a new market generally takes 18 to 24 months. We do not promise to go faster. We make sure every month counts.
