Frequently asked questions

Crossing the Röstigraben: your questions.

What is the Röstigraben, and why does it matter in business?

It is the cultural border between French- and German-speaking Switzerland. Beyond language, buying habits, decision-making and professional networks differ. An offer that works in Lausanne often has to be rethought for Zurich or Basel.

Can you prospect in German-speaking Switzerland in French?

For a few contacts, sometimes. To build real business, no: German-speaking decision-makers expect conversations in German and materials designed for their codes, not just a translation. We prepare the approach and introduce you to the right people.

How can a German-speaking Swiss company grow in French-speaking Switzerland?

By adapting its message to the French-speaking market, closer to French culture in tone and relationships, and by relying on local partners. We identify the decision-makers and networks in French-speaking Switzerland that matter for your sector.

Do I need an address in the other language region?

Not at first. A presence is built through clients first. An address or a local team makes sense later, when the business calls for it.
